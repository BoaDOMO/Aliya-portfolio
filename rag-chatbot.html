What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an AI technique where a chatbot answers questions only using information from a knowledge base you provide. It does not hallucinate or make things up — every answer is grounded in your documents.

This Demo

This page lets you customize an AI assistant for a fictional company (Nova Finance). You can edit the assistant's persona and knowledge base in Console mode, then test it in Preview mode.

  • Edit the Persona to change how the AI behaves.
  • Edit About, Products, FAQ, Policies to update what the AI knows.
  • Switch to Preview to chat with your assistant.
  • Click Save to persist your changes.

Try asking

What are your loan interest rates? How do I apply? What documents do I need? Can I repay early?

Architecture

When you send a message, the backend embeds your question, searches the knowledge base for the most relevant passages, and feeds them to Gemini along with the persona instructions. This is the same pattern used in production enterprise AI assistants.
